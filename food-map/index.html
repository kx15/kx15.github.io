    All spots → one Google map

    Google doesn't let other apps write to your saved lists, but it does let you import a file into Google My Maps. That map then shows up in the Google Maps app under Saved → Maps.

    1. Download KML below.
    2. Open google.com/maps/d → Create a new map.
    3. On the layer, click Import and pick the file. Each pin carries the Instagram link and your notes.
    4. On your phone: Google Maps → You / Saved → Maps.

    Re-importing later? Delete the old layer in My Maps first so pins don't double up.

    One spot at a time

    Each spot has an Open in Google Maps button. Tap it, then hit Save in Google Maps to drop it into any list (Want to go, Favourites…).

    Backup & move devices

    Spots are stored in this browser only. Back them up, or move them to your phone.

    Share straight from Instagram

    Install this page as an app (Android Chrome: ⋮ → Add to Home screen) and it appears in Instagram's share sheet. On iPhone, use Share → Copy link, then paste it here.
